## Editorial methodology

- Dates follow the local calendar day of the event. Korean dramas and variety shows use the Korean premiere date; overseas screenings, streaming releases, uploads and publication dates follow their respective sources.
- Start and end dates describe the run of a work or season. Individual appearances specify episode numbers when known. Nonconsecutive episodes are recorded separately. Personal participation dates take precedence when known.
- Approximate dates or ranges reflect recollections, approximate months or known upper bounds. Verification labels identify missing credits, episode numbers or filming dates. Scheduled entries refer to official future plans as of the verification date.
- Confirmed participation with missing details remains in the main timeline; unconfirmed participation, magazine issues or credits belong in a separate section. A release date is not necessarily a personal participation date.
- Coverage includes screen works and spin-offs, variety and hosting, music and music videos, filming, public awards and selected brand events. Production companies, broadcasters, original media, interviews and institutional archives take priority, with contemporary reporting and multilingual databases used for cross-checks. Early independent films, one-off events and removed archives may be missing.
