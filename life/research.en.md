Following her agency profile and contemporary reporting, this archive treats **episode 10 of The Producers, broadcast on 2015-06-13**, as her formal television acting debut. Earlier material includes her trainee years, school performances, and leads concerning early pictorials and music videos. ## How to read this archive

- Unless otherwise specified, dates follow the local calendar day where the event took place. Drama and Korean variety-show dates refer to their first Korean broadcast. Overseas theatrical premieres, streaming releases, video uploads and news publication dates follow their respective sources and are identified in each entry.
- The first and last dates of a complete drama or season describe the work’s broadcast run. Individual cameos and guest appearances include episode numbers where possible; two separate episodes are listed with a comma between their dates. Where her own period of participation is known, that individual period is used.
- **〔Approximate date or interval〕** covers retrospective recollections, dates known only to the month, and known upper bounds. **Labels containing “unverified”** identify the specific detail requiring confirmation, such as an appearance credit, episode number or filming date. **〔Announced〕** identifies an official schedule still forthcoming at the source review date.
- **Records awaiting verification are handled separately:** confirmed participation with a missing episode number or individual date remains in the main timeline, with the gap identified. Records without independent evidence of participation, a particular magazine issue or a credit are moved to the research section. A work’s release date is not the same as the date of an individual’s participation.
- Coverage includes films, dramas and spin-offs, variety shows and hosting, music videos and music, filming, public awards, and selected brand activities. Priority is given to producers, broadcasters, the works themselves, interviews with the people involved, and records from the responsible institutions; contemporary reporting and multilingual databases provide cross-checks. Coverage is limited to public material accessible during this review. Early independent shorts, one-off events and regional archives that have been removed may still be missing.

English titles follow the linked release materials where available. Where an official English title has not been established, a Korean title or a descriptive rendering is used. Notes about an “official Chinese title” refer to the title metadata in the original Chinese archive.
